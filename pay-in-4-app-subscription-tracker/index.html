What is EZER?

By Chiagozie Oliver, founder of EZER · Updated Nov 18, 2026

EZER is a subscription tracker app for Android that finds the recurring charges on your cards and warns you before they bill. It is live on Google Play in the US, and the EZER card with Pay in 4 is coming soon, with a waitlist in the app.

What works today

  • Connects to your bank through Plaid (read-only) and lists every recurring charge: streaming, apps, gyms, software, and free trials about to convert.
  • Renewal alerts a few days before a charge, and a warning before a free trial turns into a bill.
  • Price-change tracking on your subscriptions.
  • For each subscription, the cancel page and the steps to get there.
  • Wallet: how much each card spent this month, this year or any range, merchant by merchant.

What is coming soon

Coming soon The EZER card with Pay in 4. The plan: split card purchases into 4 interest-free payments over 6 weeks, 25% today, then every 2 weeks. The card program with Highnote and its sponsor bank is not live yet, so it is waitlist only. Joining the waitlist issues no card, extends no credit and collects no payment details.

Coming soon Savings goals. No funds are held today.

Price

Free to download. EZER Premium is $7.99 a month after a 7-day free trial. You can cancel any time in your store account settings.

Who should use something else

  • You need to split a purchase right now: EZER's Pay in 4 card is still coming soon (waitlist). Klarna, Afterpay, Affirm and PayPal offer it today. See the best pay in 4 apps in 2026, where EZER is listed as coming soon.
  • You want someone to cancel for you or negotiate your bills: EZER does neither. See EZER vs Rocket Money.
  • You're on iPhone: the iOS app is in review, not on the App Store yet.

Privacy

EZER reads transactions through Plaid with read-only access. No ads. You can delete your account and data any time from Settings. EZER is a financial technology company, not a bank. Built solo by Chiagozie Oliver (Chiagozie LLC dba EZER, Parker, Texas).

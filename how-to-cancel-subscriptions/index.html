How to find and cancel all your subscriptions

By Chiagozie Oliver, founder of EZER · Updated Nov 18, 2026

Check four places: Google Play, Apple, your card statements, and each merchant's account page. EZER does the card statement part for you on Android in the US: it lists every recurring charge and shows the cancel page and steps for each one (Get EZER on Google Play).

1. Google Play

Open Google Play subscriptions while signed in to the Google Account that pays, pick the subscription and cancel. Uninstalling an app does not cancel its subscription (Google's own help page).

2. Apple

On iPhone: Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Pick the one to cancel.

Sources (checked Nov 18 2026): https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7018481, https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428

3. Your card and bank statements

Subscriptions you paid for on a website don't show up in Google Play or Apple. Look through the last few months of each card for charges that repeat: streaming, gyms, software, and free trials that turned into a bill. This is the part EZER automates. It connects through Plaid (read-only), lists every recurring charge and warns you before renewals and trial conversions.

4. Cancel at the merchant

Sign in to each service and cancel from its account or billing page. In EZER, every subscription has its cancel page and the steps to get there. EZER does not cancel for you. If you want that, see EZER vs Rocket Money.

Is the FTC click to cancel rule in effect?

No. The FTC's 2024 click to cancel rule (an update to its Negative Option Rule) was vacated by a federal appeals court. On March 11, 2026 the FTC asked for public comment on next steps, including "adopting provisions of the vacated 2024 Rule or some other provisions" (FTC press release, checked Nov 18 2026). Until a new rule lands, each company still sets its own cancel flow, so keep the cancel steps handy.

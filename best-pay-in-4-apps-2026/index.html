1. PayPal

Best for $0 late fees. PayPal says it charges $0 in late fees on this plan. 4 interest-free payments, 2 weeks apart, the first at purchase. Purchases of $10 to $2,000. A soft credit check may be needed and won't affect your score. Not available in MO.

Sources (checked Nov 18 2026): https://www.paypal.com/us/digital-wallet/ways-to-pay/buy-now-pay-later

2. Affirm

Best if you may need longer than 6 weeks. 0% APR on its 4-payment option. Its example: 4 interest-free payments every 2 weeks. Pay-over-time plans run 0 to 36% APR based on credit. Checking for plans won't affect your credit, per Affirm.

Sources (checked Nov 18 2026): https://www.affirm.com/disclosures

3. Klarna

Best for a card that works anywhere Visa is accepted. 4 interest-free payments every 2 weeks, the first when your order ships. Late fee up to $7.00, total capped at 25% of the order. Soft credit check.

Sources (checked Nov 18 2026): https://www.klarna.com/us/pay-in-4/

4. Afterpay

Best for a clear late-fee cap. 25% now, then 3 payments every 2 weeks, no interest. Late fee up to $8.00 after 10 days, never more than 25% of the order. Pay Monthly runs 0.00% to 35.99% APR.

Sources (checked Nov 18 2026): https://www.afterpay.com/en-US/installment-agreement

5. Zip

Best if your state sets a lower late-fee cap. 4 biweekly payments, the first due today. Fees vary by purchase ($0 to $140) and show at checkout. Late fee $7, or less where your state sets a lower amount.

Sources (checked Nov 18 2026): https://zip.co/us/loan-terms-of-service

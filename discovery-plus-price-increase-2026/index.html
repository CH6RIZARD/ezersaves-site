Discovery+ price increase 2026: what you pay now and how to cancel

By Chiagozie Oliver, founder of EZER · Updated Nov 21, 2026

Discovery+ went up $1 to $6.99 a month with ads and $2 to $11.99 a month ad-free, plus tax. New subscribers have paid the new price since October 13, 2026. If you already subscribe, discovery+ says you're charged the new price "on your next billing date on or after November 12, 2026", so November and December bills are the first higher ones for most people. To cancel, use discovery+ Account, then Subscription, or the app store or provider that bills you. When your bill changes

  • New subscribers: since October 13, 2026.
  • Existing subscribers billed by discovery+: your next billing date on or after November 12, 2026.
  • Billed through Amazon, Apple, Google Play, Roku, Samsung, DIRECTV, Sling TV or another provider: discovery+ says that provider decides how it tells you, so check your statement.

How to cancel or switch plans

  • Billed by discovery+: Account, then Subscription, then cancel.
  • Billed by an app store: discovery+ says changes must be made on the app store. Google Play: Google Play subscriptions. iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions.
  • Roku: my.roku.com, then your purchase history. Samsung: samsungcheckout.com, Purchase History, Subscriptions.
  • Want to stay but pay less? The plan with ads is $5 a month less than Ad-Free at the new prices ($6.99 vs $11.99).

Not sure who bills you? Look at the charge on your card. If it says Apple, Google, Amazon or Roku, cancel there.

It's not only discovery+

Parks Associates says the average entry-level monthly price across more than 300 US streaming services is now $11.08, up 8% from $10.20 in Q4 2022, and 54% of US internet households subscribe to five or more paid streaming services (Parks Associates via Indian Television, Nov 19 2026). With that many services, a $1 or $2 raise is easy to miss on a statement.

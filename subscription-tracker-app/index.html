Subscription tracker or subscription manager app: what's the difference?

Mostly the name. A tracker shows what you pay for. A manager also helps you act on it: a warning before a charge, the price going up, the way out. Ask three things of any subscription manager app: does it find subscriptions on its own (from your bank) or make you type them in, does it warn you before a renewal or free trial bills, and does it cancel for you or show you how. EZER finds them from your bank, warns you before they bill and shows the cancel page and steps. It does not cancel for you; Rocket Money Premium does. Not sure what you're paying for yet?

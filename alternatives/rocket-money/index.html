Rocket Money alternative in 2026: EZER vs Rocket Money (Truebill)

By Chiagozie Oliver, founder of EZER · Updated Nov 18, 2026

EZER is a Rocket Money alternative for Android in the US: it lists your subscriptions, alerts you before they bill and shows you how to cancel each one, for $7.99 a month after a 7-day free trial (Get EZER on Google Play). If you want a team to cancel for you or negotiate your bills, Rocket Money does that and EZER does not.

Is Rocket Money the same as Truebill?

Yes. Rocket Money used to be Truebill. Its Google Play listing still runs under the app ID com.truebill (https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.truebill).

When Rocket Money is the better pick

  • You want someone else to do the cancelling.
  • You want bill negotiation (Premium+).
  • You're on iPhone today. EZER's iOS app is still in review.
  • You want a tracker that costs nothing. Rocket Money's free plan includes subscription tracking.

When EZER fits

  • You want a flat $7.99 a month with no negotiation fees.
  • You want renewal alerts, free trial warnings and price-change tracking, with cancel steps for each subscription.
  • You want to see what each card spent this month, merchant by merchant (Wallet).
  • You want Pay in 4 from the same app later. It's coming soon, join the waitlist in the app.

What EZER does today

  • Connects to your bank through Plaid (read-only) and lists every recurring charge: streaming, apps, gyms, software, and free trials about to convert.
  • Renewal alerts a few days before a charge, and a warning before a free trial turns into a bill.
  • Price-change tracking on your subscriptions.
  • For each subscription, the cancel page and the steps to get there.
  • Wallet: how much each card spent this month, this year or any range, merchant by merchant.

FAQ

What is a good Rocket Money alternative?
EZER, if you're on Android in the US and you're fine cancelling yourself with the steps shown in the app. It costs $7.99 a month after a 7-day free trial. Get EZER on Google Play.
Is Rocket Money free?
Rocket Money has a free plan that includes subscription tracking, spend reviews and bill reminders. Cancelling for you is a Premium feature (checked Nov 18 2026).
What happened to Truebill?
Truebill is now Rocket Money. Its Google Play listing still uses the app ID com.truebill.
How much is Rocket Money Premium?
A sliding scale you choose. Rocket Money's own Jan 2026 article puts it at typically $7 to $14 a month. Premium+ is $15 a month plus tax (checked Nov 18 2026).
Does EZER cancel subscriptions for me?
No. For each subscription EZER shows you the cancel page and the steps to get there. You do the cancel yourself.
Is EZER a pay in 4 app?
Not yet. The EZER card with Pay in 4 is coming soon and is waitlist only. When it launches, the plan is 4 interest-free payments over 6 weeks: 25% today, then every 2 weeks. Joining the waitlist issues no card, extends no credit and collects no payment details.

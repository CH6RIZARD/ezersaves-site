FAQ

What is a good Klarna alternative?
For splitting a purchase today: Afterpay, Affirm or PayPal (see the 2026 comparison). EZER is an alternative for tracking subscriptions now, and its Pay in 4 card is coming soon with a waitlist in the app.
What is Klarna's late fee?
Klarna's US Pay in 4 page (coming soon at EZER, live at Klarna) says a missed payment that fails twice is added to your next payment with a late fee of up to $7.00. Total late fees never exceed 25% of your order value (checked Nov 18 2026).
Is EZER a pay in 4 app?
Not yet. The EZER card with Pay in 4 is coming soon and is waitlist only. When it launches, the plan is 4 interest-free payments over 6 weeks: 25% today, then every 2 weeks. Joining the waitlist issues no card, extends no credit and collects no payment details.

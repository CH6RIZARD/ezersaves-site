Netflix price 2026: what each plan costs now

By Chiagozie Oliver, founder of EZER · Updated Nov 22, 2026

Netflix costs $8.99 a month for Standard with ads, $19.99 for Standard and $26.99 for Premium in the US, plus tax where it applies (Netflix help, checked Nov 22 2026). The last increase came on March 26, 2026, and a Comparitech study updated November 19, 2026 puts the US Standard plan up 43% in five years, from $13.99 to $19.99. When did Netflix last raise prices?

March 26, 2026, for new members. TechCrunch reported Standard with ads going from $7.99 to $8.99 and Standard from $17.99 to $19.99. Netflix said existing members "will be notified by email a month before" the new price applies to them, so the increase reached current bills over the following months.

What the new five-year study says

Comparitech's study, updated November 19, 2026, tracks the Standard plan without ads in 242 countries. Its findings:

  • US Standard went from $13.99 to $19.99 over five years, up 43%.
  • The average Standard price across all countries is $11.72, up 3% from $11.41 in March 2021.
  • Standard prices rose in 115 countries, stayed the same in 14 and fell in 113, mostly in Africa and Asia, measured in US dollars.

Prices and exchange rates in the study were checked November 9, 2026.

Sources (checked Nov 22 2026): https://www.comparitech.com/news/netflix-prices-2021-2026/

How to pay less for Netflix

  • Drop to Standard with ads: $8.99 instead of $19.99, which is $11 a month or $132 a year less.
  • Drop from Premium to Standard: $7 a month or $84 a year less.
  • Check for an extra member slot you no longer use: each is $7.99 or $9.99 a month.
  • Pause by cancelling between shows. Netflix says you keep watching until the end of the billing period you paid for.

How to cancel Netflix

Go to netflix.com/cancelplan, select Cancel, then Finish Cancellation. Netflix says deleting the app or signing out does not cancel. If you pay through a partner (a phone or TV provider, or an app store), there is no Cancel button there: cancel with that partner.

Sources (checked Nov 22 2026): https://help.netflix.com/en/node/407

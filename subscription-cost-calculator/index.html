Questions

How do I find all my subscriptions?

Check six places: your bank and card statements for charges that repeat, Google Play or iPhone subscriptions, PayPal automatic payments, Amazon Memberships and Subscriptions, and your email for receipts. Or link your bank in EZER and it lists every recurring charge on your cards for you.

How is the yearly cost worked out?

Weekly prices count 52 times a year, monthly prices 12 times, yearly prices once. Per month is the yearly total divided by 12, per day divided by 365.

Is anything I type saved?

No. The maths runs in your browser. Nothing is stored and nothing is sent anywhere.

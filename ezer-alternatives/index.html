EZER alternatives in 2026

By Chiagozie Oliver, founder of EZER · Updated Nov 21, 2026

The closest alternatives to EZER are Rocket Money, which has a free plan and a team that cancels for you on Premium, and the free subscription lists built into Google Play and Apple, which need no bank login. I built EZER, so this page says who I'd send to each of them, and who EZER is for: people on Android in the US who want every recurring charge on their cards in one list, with a warning before each one bills.

Pick something else if

  • You're on iPhone. EZER's iOS app is in review and not on the App Store yet. Rocket Money is on iOS, and Apple lists your App Store subscriptions under Settings, your name, Subscriptions.
  • You're outside the US. EZER is US only.
  • You want someone else to do the cancelling. Rocket Money's team does it on Premium. EZER shows you the cancel page and the steps, and you do the cancel yourself.
  • You want your bills negotiated. That's Rocket Money Premium+. EZER doesn't do it.
  • You don't want to pay anything. Rocket Money's free plan includes subscription tracking, and the Google Play and Apple lists are free.
  • You won't link a bank account. Use the Google Play or Apple list. They only show their own store's subscriptions, so anything you signed up for on a website won't be there.
  • You want budgets or net worth. EZER does neither. Intuit says Mint's transaction review, spending monitoring and net worth tracking "now have a new home" on Credit Karma (mint.intuit.com, checked Nov 18 2026). More on the Mint alternative page.

Where EZER fits

  • You're on Android in the US and you want every recurring charge on your cards in one list, including the ones you signed up for on a website, which the store lists miss.
  • You want a heads-up a few days before a renewal, a warning before a free trial turns into a bill, and price-change tracking.
  • You're fine cancelling yourself, as long as the cancel page and the steps are in front of you.
  • You want one flat price: $7.99 a month after a 7-day free trial, cancel any time in your store account settings.
  • You want to see what each card spent this month, this year or any range, merchant by merchant (Wallet).

FAQ

What are the best alternatives to EZER?
For subscription tracking: Rocket Money, which has a free plan and a team that cancels for you on Premium, and the free subscription lists in Google Play and Apple, which need no bank login. For budgets and net worth, Intuit points former Mint users to Credit Karma.
Is there a free alternative to EZER?
Yes. Rocket Money's free plan includes subscription tracking, spend reviews and bill reminders (checked Nov 18 2026). Google Play and Apple list your app store subscriptions for free. EZER is free to download, and Premium is $7.99 a month after a 7-day free trial.
Is there an EZER alternative for iPhone?
Rocket Money is on iOS and Android. Apple also lists your App Store subscriptions under Settings, your name, Subscriptions. EZER's iOS app is in review and is not on the App Store yet.
Which EZER alternative cancels subscriptions for me?
Rocket Money's team cancels for you on Premium. EZER shows the cancel page and the steps for each subscription, and you do the cancel yourself.
What can I use instead of EZER for pay in 4?

Who is EZER for?
People on Android in the US who want every recurring charge on their cards listed, an alert before each renewal or free trial bills, and the cancel page and steps for each subscription.
